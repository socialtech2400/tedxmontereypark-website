The theme

Tomorrow belongs to people willing to rethink it.

Reimagining Tomorrow is a day for ideas that can change how we understand our community, our choices, and what comes next.

Whether you are a student, a community member, a researcher, an artist, or someone who has simply lived through something worth talking about, we would love to hear from you.

You do not need a finished talk. Start with a clear idea worth developing.

Your idea

We are looking for the idea, and the person behind it.

Take your time. Be yourself. You do not need every detail polished before you apply.

Tell us what you are rethinking ↓
  1. Clear

    You can express the central idea in one direct sentence.

  2. Grounded

    The idea comes from research, practice, observation, or lived experience.

  3. Generous

    The audience leaves with a new way to see, question, or act.

  4. Ready to grow

Questions

Before you apply

Find quick answers about eligibility, experience, and what happens after you apply.

Who should apply?

Students, community members, researchers, artists, professionals, and people with meaningful lived experience are all invited. The idea matters more than your job title.

Do I need public speaking experience?

No. The stage is open to first-time speakers and seasoned speakers alike.

Does my idea have to be about Monterey Park?

No. Ideas may begin locally or reach far beyond the community. They should give this audience a compelling way to rethink what comes next.

What happens after I apply?

The organizing team will review applications as they arrive and contact selected applicants with next steps.

Is there a fee to speak?

No. Speakers do not pay to participate in a TEDx event.
