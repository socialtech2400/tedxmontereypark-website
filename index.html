The theme

Tomorrow belongs to people willing to rethink it.

Reimagining Tomorrow brings together ideas that challenge how we understand our community, our choices, and what comes next.

Students, community members, researchers, artists, educators, and curious neighbors are invited to spend the day listening, questioning, and connecting.

Come ready to encounter an idea that changes the way you see tomorrow.
